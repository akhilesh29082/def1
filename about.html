01 / Origin

Our
Story

The battlespace is undergoing a profound transformation. Contested skies, contested seas, contested spectrum. The nations we serve face adversaries who innovate at unprecedented speed. Meeting that challenge demands sovereign capability, not imported dependency.

Withx Defence was founded on a single conviction — that unmanned systems will save time, money and, most importantly, the lives of the men and women who serve. We design, build and sustain platforms end-to-end, from composite airframe to autonomy stack, from radar cross-section to logistics chain.

Today our systems operate across maritime, aerial and ground domains, bound together by a common mission architecture built for interoperability with allied forces. What began in a garage is now a vertically integrated house of engineering, manufacturing and sustainment.

We do not ship product.
We ship capability.

02 / Timeline

Fifteen Years.One Mission.

2011
Foundation
Withx Defence founded in Bengaluru by veterans of the armed forces and aerospace industry, with a mandate to build sovereign unmanned capability.
2014
First Flight
Sentinel MK-I completes its maiden autonomous flight — a 14-hour endurance milestone that established our aerial systems programme.
2017
Naval Charter
Marlin USV programme launched. First maritime autonomy contract signed with an allied navy, opening our surface and sub-surface division.
2020
Global Deployments
Systems enter operational service across seven nations. Aegis C2 mission software platform released to allied command centres.
2023
Composites Plant
180,000 sq. ft. manufacturing facility opens in Pune, achieving vertical integration of composites, avionics and final assembly.
2026
Next Horizon
Loyal-wingman airframe development commences under a new strategic alliance, extending our reach into next-generation air dominance.
