    OUR STACK

    CORE TECHNOLOGIES.
    ENGINEERED IN-HOUSE.

    Artificial Intelligence

    Our AI-powered defence systems enable autonomous target detection, real-time object tracking, intelligent flight navigation, and mission planning. By processing sensor and camera data on-board, our drones deliver faster situational awareness, reduce operator workload, and enhance decision-making during surveillance and reconnaissance missions.

    Autonomous Systems

    Advanced mission planning and autonomous flight capabilities enable rapid deployment, precise navigation, and reliable operation in complex tactical environments.

    Embedded Systems

    Mission-grade flight control systems and optimized onboard software deliver stable flight performance, precise navigation, and reliable operation in demanding defence environments.

    Computer Vision

    We leverage advanced computer vision and AI-powered image processing to enable real-time object detection, target tracking, aerial surveillance, and enhanced situational awareness, providing defence drones with intelligent reconnaissance and mission support capabilities.

    Internet of Things

    Our IoT-enabled communication systems provide secure telemetry, real-time video transmission, and continuous data exchange between unmanned aerial platforms and ground control stations. By integrating smart sensors and connected devices, we enable real-time situational awareness, remote monitoring, and efficient mission coordination for surveillance and reconnaissance operations.

    Secure Communication Systems

    We develop IoT-enabled secure communication systems that provide encrypted telemetry, real-time data transmission, and reliable command-and-control connectivity between unmanned aerial platforms and ground control stations, ensuring seamless mission coordination and operational awareness.

    Drone & UAV Technologies

    We design and develop advanced unmanned aerial systems (UAS) that deliver autonomous flight, real-time surveillance, reconnaissance, border monitoring, and intelligence gathering, providing defence organizations with reliable mission-ready aerial capabilities.

    Robotics & Automation

    We build robotic systems capable of performing hazardous, repetitive, and precision-based operations, reducing risk to personnel and improving operational efficiency.

    Mechanical Engineering

    Our mechanical engineering team develops advanced UAV airframes, precision structural components, and mission-ready drone platforms engineered for durability, stability, and reliable performance in demanding defence and security operations.

    Electronics & PCB Design

    We design advanced PCB solutions and embedded electronic systems that integrate flight controllers, sensors, communication modules, navigation systems, and power management, ensuring reliable performance and seamless operation in mission-ready defence drones.

    Sensor Fusion

    By integrating data from advanced payloads and onboard systems—including EO/IR cameras, radar, GPS, IMUs, LiDAR, and environmental sensors—we enable precise situational awareness, real-time target tracking, and intelligent autonomous decision-making for mission-critical defence operations.

    Cloud & Edge Computing

    Our defence drone solutions combine powerful onboard edge computing with secure networked systems to support real-time data processing, mission coordination, fleet monitoring, operational analytics, and dependable information management in mission-critical environments.
